# Art and attribution

`assets/ashford.png` is original AI-generated environment scenery made with the built-in image-generation tool. It is used for the title screen and battle backdrop. Characters, creature silhouettes, city geometry, and effects are original code-rendered assets in `art.js` and `adventure.js`. No Final Fantasy art, music, characters, or logo is included. The project takes inspiration from the readable party-command structure of classic JRPGs.

## Final environment prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished environment background for an original playable JRPG, Mutant Odyssey. Create a cinematic wide landscape of Ashford, a hopeful rain-washed near-future city at blue hour: elevated luminous glass Helix institute in the distance, art-deco brick towers, copper pipework, cyan energy conduits, a distant pale orbital ring in the sky, warm amber windows. Detailed realistic 3D game environment rendering, polished dramatic lighting, beautiful atmospheric depth, not pixel art. Camera from street level across a broad empty plaza; lower third is unobstructed dark wet stone paving where separately animated battle characters will stand. Buildings frame the sides, distant central avenue. No people, no creatures, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Landscape 16:9.
